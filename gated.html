Complex Compression Networks & Metric Audits

Evaluating multi-stage pure-compression-optimization networks (no resize, crop, or artistic transforms), 50% size-budget targets, 3 effort tiers, and Butteraugli / DSSIM cross-metric checks on a stratified panel across all 12 categories.

Strict anti-cheating rule: Every pre-conditioned chain (denoise → codec, posterize → quantette → oxipng → ect) is scored with alpha-aware SSIMULACRA2 v2.1 against the unfiltered original pixels — never against the pre-filtered intermediate.

1. Complex Pure-Compression-Optimization Networks vs. Single-Pass Baselines

Positive lift = smaller file at the same or higher SSIMULACRA2 v2.1 floor. Negative lift = the extra stage hurt compression or forced a higher bitrate to recover original detail.

2. Additional Target: 50% Size Budget (≤ 50% of Original Bytes)

Instead of fixing quality and measuring size, we fix the budget at 50% of original bytes and measure the highest SSIMULACRA2 v2.1 quality each main web format achieves.

3. Effort-Tier Efficiency (Fast vs. Balanced vs. Thorough)

Measured honestly in wall-clock seconds per megapixel and KB saved per second on the stratified panel.

4. Cross-Metric Finalist Audit (SSIMULACRA2 vs. Butteraugli & DSSIM)

No single perceptual metric is infallible. We cross-check balanced winners from every category with native Butteraugli (lower is better; < 1.0 good, > 2.0 noticeable) and DSSIM (lower is better).
