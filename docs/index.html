How to read these numbers

Alpha-aware SSIMULACRA2 v2.1
Every transparent image is composited over white, black, and mid-gray backgrounds and scored with the worst (lowest) of the three. 100 = pixel-identical; 90+ = visually lossless even zoomed in; 80+ = high quality (identical at normal viewing size); 70+ = good for web; 50+ = Extreme / Aggressive (strong compression for thumbnails, mobile galleries, and heavy bandwidth savings).
Byte-weighted vs. per-image mean
Byte-weighted saving measures total transfer bytes saved across the corpus (what your CDN bill sees). Mean saving weights every image equally regardless of resolution.
Main web formats
Auto Web picks the smallest passing file among the 4 universally supported web formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, and AVIF. JXL is also benchmarked alongside as a next-generation reference.
