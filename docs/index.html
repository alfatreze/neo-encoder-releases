About the times. A megapixel is a 1000×1000 picture; most phone photos are 8–12 megapixels, so scale up accordingly. Times were measured on one Mac with two encodes running at once and will differ on your machine. Fast only allows the quickest recipes, up to about 3× the time of the very quickest. How to read these numbers

File size saving
How much smaller the file gets. A 50% saving cuts a 1 MB image to 500 KB (half the download); a 90% saving cuts it to 100 KB.
Quality levels
Visually lossless (S2 ≥ 90): invisible even zoomed in. High quality (S2 ≥ 80): looks identical at normal viewing size. Good for web (S2 ≥ 70): crisp for web pages, with minor softening only on close inspection.
Two datasets on this site
The 100,000-trial sweep below used fast SSIM to compare recipe chains; the stricter human-vision results (SSIMULACRA2) and newer encoders (cjpegli, quantette, AVIF) are on the SSIMULACRA2 and Explore pages. Full method on Methodology.
