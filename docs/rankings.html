Chain rankings

Which chains of encoding steps work best at each effort level. Choose all images for a general answer, or a type of image for a specific one.

How to read the ranking. The big number is the average size saving. One recipe for all images is what you get with a single default recipe from that chain (its best-performing setting). Best recipe per image shows the ceiling if every image got its own best settings from that chain; the paler part of the bar marks that ceiling. Wins is the share of images where this chain gave the smallest result. Time compares with the quickest recipe.
Technical note. Results are judged by NEO's SSIM similarity score at 0.995, 0.98 and 0.95 for the three quality levels, and effort is time relative to the fastest recipe on each image (Fast ≤ 3×, Balanced ≤ 15×, Thorough unlimited). A chain whose recipes all exceed an effort limit cannot qualify at that effort and shows 0%.
