How to read it. Each dot is one recipe on this image. Further left = smaller file; higher up = better visual quality. Large dots joined by the dashed line are the best trade-offs. Hover any dot for exact size, score and speed.

Reading the table. Each cell shows the compressed size (hover for exact bytes), percentage saved, output format badge when changed, and human-vision score (SSIMULACRA2: 100 = pixel-identical, 90+ = visually lossless, 80+ = looks identical at normal size, 70+ = good for web). ImageOptim is the real Mac app; NEO (matching) and NEO (lossless) match its recipes; NEO Smart (same format) uses cjpegli / quantette at High quality; NEO Smart (best format) races PNG, JPEG, WebP, JXL and AVIF.
